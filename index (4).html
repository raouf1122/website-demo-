Choosing a good website

What I check, and what you should look for on any guest post site.

Green flags

  • Content is on a clear topic close to yours
  • Real visitors and real readers, not only metrics
  • Pages are indexed in Google
  • Articles are edited and the site looks maintained
  • Few outgoing links per article

Red flags

  • Every topic under the sun on one site
  • Hundreds of paid posts with links to unrelated businesses
  • Thin, badly written articles
  • No contact details or about page
  • Traffic that dropped sharply or never existed

Guest posting guides

Eight short guides on how guest posting works. Tap a title to open it.

1. What is guest posting?

Guest posting means writing an article and publishing it on someone else’s website. The site gets fresh content. You get exposure to its readers and, usually, a link back to your own site. It works best when the article is useful and the website’s topic matches yours.

2. How does guest posting work, step by step?
  • Pick a topic and a target website
  • Check the site’s niche and rules
  • Send or write a suitable article
  • The site reviews and may edit it
  • It goes live and you receive the URL
3. Why guest posting helps SEO

Links from relevant, trusted websites are one of the signals search engines use. A good guest post can also bring referral visitors and make your brand known. No one can honestly promise a ranking, because many other factors matter too, but relevant links remain a normal part of SEO.

4. How to choose the right website

Start with relevance: does the site cover your topic? Then check that it has real readers, that its pages appear in Google, and that its articles look edited. A smaller site in the right niche is often better than a large site on random topics.

5. Dofollow, nofollow and sponsored links

A dofollow link passes ranking credit. Nofollow and sponsored tags tell search engines not to count it fully. Google asks that paid links carry a sponsored tag, so many sites use it. Ask for the link type before you order, and value the readers a link brings too.

6. Using anchor text safely

Anchor text is the clickable words of a link. Natural anchors such as your brand name, your website address or a plain phrase look normal. Repeating the same exact keyword in every post looks forced and can attract unwanted attention.

7. What makes a good guest post article
  • Original, never copied or spun
  • Answers a real question for the reader
  • Clear headings and short paragraphs
  • Link placed where it helps the reader
  • Follows the site’s guidelines
8. Common guest posting mistakes

Posting on link farms, using irrelevant sites, stuffing keywords, reusing one article on many sites, and picking a site only for its numbers. Fewer, well-matched placements usually do more than a pile of weak ones.
